Guiding principles for technical work

  1. Understand the part and the process

    Start right at the spindle, the drawing, and the people running the machines. True constraints aren't always in the CAD model.

  2. Measure before changing

    Rely on rigorous data for cycle times, scrap rates, setup durations, and tooling wear rather than gut instinct.

  3. Make changes practical

    Recommend strategies and tooling that the shop floor can reliably manufacture, operate, inspect, and maintain.

  4. Document and hand off

    Ensure setup sheets, inspection plans, and standard operating procedures empower operators for sustainable consistency.
